# Phase 20 experiment

**Hypothesis:** multiple horizons, filing-date SEC fundamentals, sector-neutral shrinkage and fee gates may improve four-metric scores relative to initial equal80 HOLD.

**Inputs:** complete historical hourly dataset; Phase19 cached CompanyFacts (as of retrieval).

**Design:** expanding-year fit; 2023/2024 development; 2025 previously exposed; 2026 untouched. 15d non-overlapping windows.

**Limitations:** hourly fill proxies, SEC filed-date+2d proxy, no validated historical news, 2025 not independent; no Official deployment.

**Decision:** promote only after examining year-by-year four-metric scores, leakage and live interface.
